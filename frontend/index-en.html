EVALUATION NOTES

What do these numbers prove — and what not?

The test compares detection of unsupported trust transfer against a simple keyword baseline. Some examples are derived from similar templates, which may inflate performance. We have not validated the system on independent real-world Jordanian data or trained a language model.

You can reproduce the benchmark locally: python -m scripts.evaluate. The included results are reproducible while the rules and dataset remain unchanged.

PROJECT / WADI RUM

From Wadi Rum to digital security.

A Jordanian technology experiment challenging the illusion that appearing trustworthy is the same as being verified.

WADI RUM · JORDAN

What looks true is not always proof.

THE CONNECTION

Why Wadi Rum?

Inspired by a mirage: what appears real is not by itself proof. Likewise, mentioning a real institution does not make a link or verification-code request legitimate. This is an educational analogy, not a physical technology.

HERITAGE FACT

Jordanian heritage

Wadi Rum was listed as a UNESCO mixed natural and cultural World Heritage Site in 2011. Its rock carvings and inscriptions document a long human history.

Official source: UNESCO ↗
TECHNICAL DISCLOSURE

Technical limitations

The project does not visit URLs, check reputation, authenticate institutions, or claim to use a trained AI model. It runs transparent testable rules, which can make false positive and false negative errors.

PRIVACY & SAFETY

Privacy first

No accounts and no database storing submitted messages. Use redacted text and never include real secrets or personal data. Findings are educational, not a definitive security verdict.

RESEARCH / HOW IT WORKS

How the engine works

The engine extracts sensitive requests, claims and links, then visualizes their relationships. The URL Inspector checks URL structure locally without visiting or validating a site.

ETHICS / SECURITY

When not to rely on the output

False positives and negatives are possible. Do not submit secrets or treat a low score as a safety certificate. Verify institutions using independently known official channels.
